## How to interpret timing

- **≤ 0.75× planned:** already familiar; keep moving.
- **0.75×–1.5× planned:** expected range.
- **1.5×–2.5× planned:** mark for revision.
- **> 2.5× planned:** stop optimizing for speed; identify the exact conceptual blocker.

Timing is diagnostic, not a score.
